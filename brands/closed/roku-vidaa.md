# Roku / Hisense VIDAA — settings + DNS only

These platforms are closed: no ADB, no sideloaded launcher, no package
management from a PC.

- What works: privacy settings on the TV (ACR/viewing data OFF,
  personalized ads OFF) + DNS-level blocking on the LAN.
- What does not work: debloat lists, replacement launchers, remote
  package control. Any guide claiming otherwise is wrong.
